## Who we are

The Manchester AI Guild is a not-for-profit. We help businesses in Greater Manchester and the North West adopt AI in practical and ethical ways.

We bring together business, academia, government and the AI community, so that AI work gets done here and is not just talked about.

## Our purpose

The Manchester AI Guild is a not-for-profit dedicated to making AI work for the benefit of Manchester and the UK.

We advise SMEs and institutions on their technology needs, grow people's understanding and skills, and connect to service providers. We strive to help businesses solve their challenges through impartial and practical advice, building on decades of experience and a shared knowledge base, to drive lasting change in Manchester and the UK through the responsible adoption of AI.

We have two ambitions:

1. Help SMEs and enterprises adopt AI to drive growth, improve efficiency and solve real business problems.
2. Bring business, academia, government and the AI community together to build the next generation of AI capability to support that growth.

## What we offer

- **SME Workshops.** An afternoon workshop, a workshop for one company, or an open session. Self-sponsored: your business funds its own place.
- **AI Consultancy.** Practical help to apply AI safely in your organisation.

### The Manchester Way

The Manchester Way is the Guild's practical method for helping small businesses adopt AI: it looks at a business like a band and helps you find where to start.

**Is this only for generative AI?** No. Generative AI is a major focus, but the guidance applies across AI systems.
