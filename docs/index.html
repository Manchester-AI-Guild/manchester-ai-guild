Making AI work for good in Manchester

The Manchester AI Guild is a private limited company. We help businesses adopt AI safely and usefully, and we connect the people, companies, universities and public bodies that make Manchester an AI city.

Objective one

Help businesses adopt AI safely: start with strategy and governance, then data, technology and people, so the result lasts.

Objective two

Grow the ecosystem, academia and business together, so that Manchester is recognised globally as an AI-first city.

The Manchester story

Manchester was the world's first industrial city. The atom was split here, the first stored program ran here in 1948, and graphene was isolated here. The University counts 26 Nobel laureates among its people.

That record is not yet turning into the growth it should. Of the fifty companies in Tech Nation's Future Fifty 2026, one is headquartered in Manchester. Start-ups grow here without the city noticing; small businesses do not know the university's door is open to them; AI projects start with tools rather than readiness, and governance fails before the technology does. Each of these is a gap in connection, not in talent or capital, and connecting people is what a guild is for. The Guild's job is to be the layer between the universities, the funders and the businesses that make up most of the city's economy.

Five foundations, in order

The Guild's working method for adopting AI. Five foundations, in order; each carries one honest question, and whichever a business answers worst is where the first effort goes.

  1. I
    Strategy and process. Can you say in one sentence what you want AI to do?
  2. II
    Governance, compliance and IT. Do you know what is allowed in, and who decides?
  3. III
    Data. Can someone else find the records this task needs?
  4. IV
    Technology and integration. Can the result reach the work without rekeying?
  5. V
    People and knowledge. Does someone own it, and is the knowledge shared?

The AI Cookbook

A short, plain-English guide to the Manchester Way: the five foundations, the questions to ask, and what good looks like at each stage. It underpins the Guild's workshops, the first of which ran at Venture Café Manchester in September 2026. The Cookbook is being published through the main Guild site.

Who we serve

The Guild connects the people and organisations that make Manchester an AI city, and helps each of them adopt AI the Manchester Way.

Businesses and SMEs

Small and medium-sized businesses that want to adopt AI safely, starting with readiness rather than tools.

Founders and entrepreneurs

People building AI products and companies in Greater Manchester who need connections, peers and a route to funders.

Professionals and practitioners

Developers, data and governance specialists who want to share knowledge and work to a common standard.

Universities and academia

Researchers and innovation hubs turning Manchester's research strength into adoption across the city.

Public and community bodies

Civic organisations and community groups making sure the ecosystem includes everyone building in it.

How to get involved

The Guild is for businesses, entrepreneurs, professionals, educational institutions and community organisations in Greater Manchester and the wider North West, and for anyone whose work or community can benefit from AI adopted responsibly.
