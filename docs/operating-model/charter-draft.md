## Working principles

- Value comes from practical delivery, not hype.
- AI adoption must remain grounded in organisational need and measurable benefit.
- Governance and risk are part of implementation, not afterthoughts.
- Members bring trusted expertise and service capability.
- The Guild is inclusive, useful and commercially realistic.
- AI that is responsible, robust, reliable and useful.

## Manchester AI Guild Oath (DRAFT — awaiting SLT approval)

> **DRAFT. This oath has not been adopted. It is synthesised from the working principles above and the Code of Conduct values, for SLT review. Do not present it as agreed.**

1. I will put practical delivery ahead of hype.
2. I will ground every AI adoption in real organisational need and measurable benefit.
3. I will treat governance and risk as part of the work, not an afterthought.
4. I will bring my trusted expertise honestly, and seek help when I reach my limits.
5. I will share knowledge freely and recognise the work of others.
6. I will put the interests of the communities we serve ahead of personal advantage.
7. I will speak up when something falls short, and listen when someone raises a concern.
