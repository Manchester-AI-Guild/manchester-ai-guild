# Purpose statement

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** FINAL. Agreed by the Manchester AI Guild Senior Leadership Team (SLT) on 11 September 2026.
**Live source:** [manchesteraiguild.org](https://www.manchesteraiguild.org/) and the Guild's [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/manchester-ai-guild).

---

The Manchester AI Guild is a not-for-profit built to make AI work for good in Manchester and the UK. We grow people, strengthen solution providers and connect the Manchester ecosystem to drive lasting change through the responsible adoption of AI.

## Our two missions

1. **Help businesses adopt AI.** Help SMEs and enterprises adopt AI to drive growth, improve efficiency and solve real business problems.
2. **Build Manchester's AI capability.** Bring business, academia, government and the AI community together to build the next generation of AI capability, solutions and talent needed to support that growth.

Build capability. Create opportunity. Grow prosperity.

Make Manchester a place where AI is not just talked about, it gets done.
