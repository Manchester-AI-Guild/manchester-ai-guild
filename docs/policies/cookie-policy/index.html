1. Purpose and scope

This policy says what manchesteraiguild.org stores on your device, why, and how you can control it. It covers every page of the website. That includes the join page and the Cookbook slide deck.

Defined terms

Cookie: a small text file a website stores in your browser.

Similar technologies: anything else that stores or reads information on your device. Examples are local storage, and scripts that fingerprint your browser.

Strictly necessary: storage the service you asked for cannot work without. It needs no consent.

2. Background

The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) set the rules for storing information on a device. Regulation 6 says a person must not store information on your device, or read information already stored there, unless an exception applies. The exceptions are in Schedule A1.

The Guild's site is built to store nothing beyond what is strictly necessary. Where the bot check processes your IP address, the lawful basis is legitimate interests (security). The Privacy notice, section 4, sets this out.

3. Principles

  • Store nothing on a visitor's device unless the site cannot work without it.

  • Say exactly what is stored. Check it in a real browser, and record the date of the check.

  • Ask for consent before adding anything that is not strictly necessary.

4. Commitments

  • We re-check the cookie store and web storage in a real browser after every release that adds a script or embed, and record the result.

  • We add a consent step before we introduce any storage that is not strictly necessary.

  • We keep the table in section 5 accurate to the last check, and show the date of that check.

5. What this website sets

Pages on manchesteraiguild.org set no cookies and use no local or session storage. There is no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking script on any page.

We checked this on 16 September 2026. We loaded every page (home, team, resources, join, every policy page, the Cookbook deck and the 404 page) in a fresh Google Chrome profile. We then read the cookie store and web storage. We found zero cookies and zero storage keys. The same check on the live domain found no cookies set by Cloudflare's edge either. The Guild keeps a record of this check.

Because we use nothing beyond strictly necessary storage, the site shows no cookie banner. If we ever add analytics or embedded video, we will ask for consent first and update this policy.

6. Third-party requests that are not cookies

  • Google Fonts: the site's typefaces (Inter and Roboto Slab) load from Google's servers. Google receives your IP address and browser details with that request. Google's Fonts API does not set or log cookies (Google, n.d.). Google's privacy FAQ is at https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

  • Slide decks on the resources page are hosted on this site, not on a third-party service.

  • Links to third-party sites open those organisations' own sites. Those sites have their own cookie practices. These are outbound links, not cookies we set.

7. How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the Turnstile mechanism may stop the join form from working. Everything else on the site works with all cookies blocked.

  • Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647

  • Safari: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac

  • Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/manage-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-view-allow-block-delete-and-use-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d

The ICO's guidance for the public is at https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/

9. Procedure: adding anything that stores data

  1. The website maintainer describes the storage (name, purpose and duration) in the pull request.

  2. The Data Protection Lead decides whether the storage is strictly necessary. If it is not, a consent mechanism is designed first.

  3. The table in section 5 is updated in the same pull request. The storage check is re-run and recorded.

  4. The Data Protection Lead tells the Senior Leadership Team (SLT) at its next meeting.

10. Compliance

We remove any script or embed that stores data without consent at the next release. If personal data was involved, we record the incident under the Incident response procedure.

12. Review

This policy is reviewed as often as is reasonable and, as a minimum, once every 12 months. It is reviewed sooner whenever something changes that affects it, for example when a feature that stores anything on your device is added.

The Senior Leadership Team (SLT) approves every review.

Before a review goes to the Senior Leadership Team (SLT), a Manchester AI Guild member who did not draft this policy QA reviews it. The reviewer is never the author. The reviewer's name and the date of the QA review are recorded in the version history.

The version and date at the top show which version you are reading.
