1. Who we are

Manchester AI Guild ("the Guild") helps Greater Manchester organisations adopt AI responsibly. The Guild is not-for-profit. We are the data controller for the personal data in this notice. A data controller decides why and how personal data is used.

  • Legal form: Manchester AI Guild Ltd, a private company limited by guarantee (company number 17490801, incorporated 30 September 2026).

  • Registered office: 1 Southern Road, Sale, England, M33 6HP.

  • ICO registration: we are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller. Registration reference ZC266348, registered 5 October 2026, renewal due 4 October 2027. You can check this on the ICO's public register: https://ico.org.uk/ESDWebPages/Search

  • Data Protection Lead: Trevor Roberts, report@manchesteraiguild.com.

  • Reporting contact for Code of Conduct, risk and compliance matters: Edwin Luther, report@manchesteraiguild.com.

Defined terms

Personal data: information that identifies you, or could do so with other information we hold.

Processing: anything we do with personal data, such as collecting, storing, using, sharing or deleting it.

Lawful basis: the reason UK GDPR Article 6 allows us to process your data.

2. What this notice covers

This notice covers personal data we collect through manchesteraiguild.org, at Guild events, in the Guild's community channels, and in the Guild's own governance work. That includes reports, surveys and business engagement. It does not cover other organisations we link to, such as LinkedIn, Slack or Venture Café Manchester. Those organisations have their own notices.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We do not pass your details to sponsors or other members unless you ask us to make an introduction. We use these processors and services:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, runs the join form, stores submissions in a Cloudflare D1 database, and provides Turnstile bot protection.

  • Slack: the Guild's community channel. A new sign-up sends a notification to a private Guild Slack channel. It says that a sign-up happened and which part of The Manchester Way was picked. It does not include your name, email or comment.

  • LinkedIn: if you follow the Guild's page, LinkedIn processes that under its own notice.

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its typefaces from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address with the font request.

  • Fastmail: hosts the Guild's mailboxes (hello@, and report@manchesteraiguild.com).

  • GitHub: hosts the Guild's public and private repositories. No personal data is stored in them (Data governance policy).

We may also share data where the law requires it, for example with the ICO or a court.

Our record of processing activities, which UK GDPR Article 30 requires, combines the tables in this notice with the Guild's technology register. Each processor is listed in the technology register with its owner.

6. International transfers

Cloudflare, Slack, LinkedIn, GitHub and Google are based in the United States. When personal data goes to a US provider certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations (the UK-US data bridge, 2023). Otherwise we rely on the provider’s UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum. The join-form database (Cloudflare D1) is created with the Western Europe location hint. Submissions are therefore stored in Western Europe.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have these rights. Using them is free. We respond within one month of receiving your request. For complex or numerous requests we may extend that by two further months (UK GDPR Article 12A, inserted by section 76 of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025). The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) says organisations usually have one month to respond to a subject access request.

  • To be informed: this notice.

  • Access: a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and the details in sections 4 to 7.

  • Rectification: correction of inaccurate data, or completion of incomplete data.

  • Erasure: deletion of your data when we no longer need it, when you withdraw consent, or when you object and we have no overriding reason to keep it.

  • Restriction: a pause on processing while we resolve a question about accuracy or our lawful basis.

  • Portability: the data you gave us under consent, in a machine-readable form.

  • Objection: to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing. We always stop direct marketing when you object.

  • Automated decision-making: the right not to be subject to a solely automated decision with legal or similar effect. We do not make such decisions.

To use any of these rights, email the Data Protection Lead at report@manchesteraiguild.com or write to the registered office. You do not need to use any particular words. We may ask you to confirm your identity. The one-month clock pauses until we receive it. The steps we follow are in the Complaints and data rights procedure.

9. How to withdraw consent

If you ticked the box to hear from us, you can change your mind at any time. Click unsubscribe on any email we send, or email report@manchesteraiguild.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it. It does not remove you from the community Slack. You can leave Slack yourself at any time.

10. Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. The join form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, input limits and a rate limit. Submissions are stored in a Cloudflare database. Only Guild SLT members with Cloudflare access can read it. Notifications to Slack never include your name, email or free text. Secrets are held in a secrets manager, never in repositories.

If we discover a personal data breach that is likely to risk your rights, we will tell the ICO without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours. We will also tell you without undue delay (Incident response procedure).

Keep your own account secure. Use a unique password, do not share your sign-in details, and turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever a platform offers it. We never ask you for your password.

11. Children

This website and Guild membership are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. For online services offered directly to children, consent is valid from age 13 (UK GDPR Article 8(1)). If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least every 12 months, and whenever our processing changes. The version and date at the top show which version you are reading. We announce material changes on the website. Where we have your consent to email you, we also send an email.

13. How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first. Email the Data Protection Lead at report@manchesteraiguild.com and we will try to put it right. If you are not satisfied, the matter goes to Edwin Luther and then to the Guild SLT, as the Complaints and data rights procedure explains.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Online: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/

  • Phone: 0303 123 1113, or live chat on the same page

15. Review

This notice is reviewed as often as is reasonable and, as a minimum, once every 12 months. It is reviewed sooner whenever something changes that affects it, for example when our processing changes or we add a new processor.

The Senior Leadership Team (SLT) approves every review.

Before a review goes to the Senior Leadership Team (SLT), a Manchester AI Guild member who did not draft this notice QA reviews it. The reviewer is never the author. The reviewer's name and the date of the QA review are recorded in the version history.
