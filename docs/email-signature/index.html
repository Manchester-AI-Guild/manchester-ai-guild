Add it to your email

  1. Gmail: Settings, See all settings, Signature, Create new. Paste the copied footer, then Save changes.
  2. Outlook (new): Settings, Accounts, Signatures, New signature. Paste, then Save.
  3. Outlook (classic): File, Options, Mail, Signatures, New. Paste, then OK.
  4. Gmail may drop the logo and photo when you paste. If so, in the signature box use Insert image to add them again, or use Outlook.
  5. If the layout looks wrong after pasting, use Copy plain text instead.
  6. Send yourself a test and open it on your phone.

Keep the wording below the line as it is. Only your name, role, email and credentials change. The legal lines are the same for everyone.
