# Manchester AI Guild

## Our purpose

The Manchester AI Guild is a not-for-profit dedicated to making AI work for the benefit of Manchester and the UK.

We advise SMEs and institutions on their technology needs, grow people's understanding and skills, and connect to service providers. We strive to help businesses solve their challenges through impartial and practical advice, building on decades of experience and a shared knowledge base, to drive lasting change in Manchester and the UK through the responsible adoption of AI.

We have two ambitions:

1. Help SMEs and enterprises adopt AI to drive growth, improve efficiency and solve real business problems.
2. Bring business, academia, government and the AI community together to build the next generation of AI capability to support that growth.

## Why now

Recent advances in AI are creating major opportunity across products, operations, and productivity. They are also introducing new risk categories including hallucinations, prompt injection, compliance uncertainty, and governance drift.

Manchester AI Guild exists to help founders, operators, researchers, and institutions adopt AI safely and at speed through practical, use-case-led collaboration.

## Current proposed approach

- **Focus on a subset of the problem**: prioritize high-value vertical slices and deliver practical value early.
- **Leverage existing frameworks**: build on existing governance and technology risk controls rather than reinventing from scratch.
- **Threat modelling and controls**: identify concrete mitigations and assurance paths for realistic architectures and use cases.
- **Action-owner delivery**: convert discussion outputs into accountable projects, issues, and milestones.

## The Manchester Way

The Manchester Way is the Guild's practical method for helping small businesses adopt AI: it looks at a business like a band and helps you find where to start. Guides are on the [Downloads page](https://manchester-ai-guild.github.io/manchester-ai-guild/downloads/).

## Services

- **SME Workshops**: The Manchester Way workshop, an afternoon (13:00 to 17:15) for SMEs. Proposed dates: 18 November and 10 December 2026, at Ada, National College for Digital Skills.
- **AI Consultancy**: help after the workshop, including setting up AI policies and working with a company's IT team.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are you building foundation models?
No. The Guild focuses on practical adoption, governance, and implementation pathways.

### Is this only for Generative AI?
No. Generative AI is a major focus, but methods apply to broader AI systems.

### Is this only for large enterprises?
No. A core focus is enabling SMEs and startups with practical, right-sized governance and implementation support.
