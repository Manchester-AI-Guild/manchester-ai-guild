# Manchester AI Guild

Our goal is to mutually develop a practical governance framework and execution model that supports the on-boarding, development, and running of AI-based solutions across Manchester-region organisations in a safe, trustworthy, and commercially valuable way.

## Why now

Recent advances in AI are creating major opportunity across products, operations, and productivity. They are also introducing new risk categories including hallucinations, prompt injection, compliance uncertainty, and governance drift.

Manchester AI Guild exists to help founders, operators, researchers, and institutions adopt AI safely and at speed through practical, use-case-led collaboration.

## Current proposed approach

- **Focus on a subset of the problem**: prioritize high-value vertical slices and deliver practical value early.
- **Leverage existing frameworks**: build on existing governance and technology risk controls rather than reinventing from scratch.
- **Threat modelling and controls**: identify concrete mitigations and assurance paths for realistic architectures and use cases.
- **Action-owner delivery**: convert discussion outputs into accountable projects, issues, and milestones.

## The Manchester Way

The Manchester Way is the Manchester AI Guild's method for SMEs. It uses a band as the picture of a business. The core band is your product, your specialist expertise, your data, your systems, your commercial backing, your customers and their feedback. Each role comes with one plain question. The aim is the AI Supernova: the intent to amplify what makes your business exceptional through ethical, sustainable and human-centred AI. Keep what makes you distinctive, find where the performance goes wrong, and test one small change.

The core band has seven roles, in order: lead singer / songwriter, lead guitarist, bass guitarist, sound engineer, record label, audience, and reviews, applause and repeat bookings. The [README](https://github.com/Manchester-AI-Guild/manchester-ai-guild#the-manchester-way) lists each role with its question.

**AI Supernova** has positive cultural and financial impact: it pays back in people and in profit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are you building foundation models?
No. The Guild focuses on practical adoption, governance, and implementation pathways.

### Is this only for Generative AI?
No. Generative AI is a major focus, but methods apply to broader AI systems.

### Is this only for large enterprises?
No. A core focus is enabling SMEs and startups with practical, right-sized governance and implementation support.
